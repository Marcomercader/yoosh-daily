Yoosh Daily Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Yoosh Daily is a daily photo exchange between two people who pair with an invite code. The app stores the first name you enter, the days you posted, and the photos you send.

How data is used

This data is used only to sync photos between you and the person you paired with, to unlock their photo after you send yours, and to show your shared streak and past photos. Yoosh Daily has no advertising, no analytics, and does not sell data or use it for tracking.

Storage and protection

Photos are encrypted on your iPhone with a key derived from your private invite code before being stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit). Copies are also kept on each paired iPhone. Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy terms.

Deletion

Choose leave pair in the app menu to delete your photos and profile from iCloud and from your phone. You can also email yooshappsupport@gmail.com to request deletion.

Children

Yoosh Daily is not directed at children under 13.
